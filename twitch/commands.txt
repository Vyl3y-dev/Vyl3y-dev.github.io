List of available commands 😺
Feel free to try them out
More to be added soon!~
'!' goes before every command
Example: !song - shows current song
!sup - HI ✋ lemme know you're here
!luxhi @user - greet someone
!itsvy - greet Vy (streamer)
!itsviv - greet Viv (@Viv4n)
!ping - pong 🏓
!lurk - (marks you as a lurker)
!hug - give someone a huggy
!8ball - ask a question get an answer
!roll - roll a dice of your choice 2-20
!socials - linktree (the image in the first panel)
!quote - displays a random quote
!miss - logs # of missed cannons
!fifty - logs # of times last hit nexus
!first - claim to be the 1st viewer
!second - claim to be the 2nd viewer
!third - claim to be the 3rd viewer
!song - displays the song playing
!playlist - links the current playlist
!queue - add a song to the spotify queue [IN DEV]
!followage - returns how long you've been following Vy
!lc - list commands
!so - shoutout to a fellow streamer 🎉 [IN DEV]
!lastgame - League previous match history (Ranked) [IN DEV]
!matches - Summary of recent league matches (Ranked) [IN DEV]
!rank - Check out my horrible League rank on my main account 😅
